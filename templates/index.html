def is_palindrome(s: str) -> bool:
    chars = [c.lower() for c in s if c.isalnum()]
    left, right = 0, len(chars) - 1
    while left < right:
        if chars[left] != chars[right]:
            return False
        left, right = left + 1, right - 1
    return True  # "A man, a plan..." -> True

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Does it work with Zoom, Google Meet and Teams?

Yes. Put your phone near the laptop speakers, or open PrapAI on the laptop and share the meeting tab with audio. PrapAI doesn't join or change your call.

How do I know it can hear the interviewer?

Tap "Test my audio first" on the Copilot screen. For 15 seconds it shows sound levels and the words it heard, then tells you if anything needs fixing. It's free and uses no minutes.

What if I'm wearing headphones?

Then your phone can't hear the interviewer. Open PrapAI on the laptop, choose "Mic + meeting audio" and share the tab that has your call.

How fast are the answers?

The first words usually appear about two seconds after the interviewer stops talking: PrapAI waits about a second to be sure they've finished, then starts writing. The rest fills in while you say the opening line.

What is the mock interview?

An AI interviewer asks you questions out loud, including questions candidates report being asked at the company you name (found with a web search) and questions about your resume. You answer out loud, it may ask a follow-up, and at the end you get a score and a better answer for each question. It uses the same minutes as the copilot, only while the interview runs.

Can it tell me what a company usually asks, and make a prep plan?

Yes. After signing in, open "Interview prep", enter the company, role, when your interview is and (optionally) the job description. PrapAI searches interview experiences candidates shared online and shows the rounds, the questions they reported, their tips and the sources, plus questions your job description and resume make likely, clearly marked as predictions. Then it builds a prep plan that fits your time: hour by hour if the interview is today, day by day if it's later, with sleep and a mock interview included. Tap any question for a suggested answer, tick tasks as you go, or practise questions in a mock interview. It's free.

Can it tailor my resume for a job?

Yes. After signing in, open "Tailored resume", add your resume and paste the job description. PrapAI rewrites your summary and bullets in the job's language, puts the most relevant first and reorders your skills. It never adds experience, numbers or skills you don't have: any rewrite that does is automatically switched back to your original line, and missing skills are listed separately so you can add them only if they're true. You review every change, then download a clean PDF (or a .tex file for Overleaf). It's free.

What do I get after a real interview?

Tap Stop, then "Interview report": every question you were asked, the suggested answers, and three topics to revise. You can copy or download it, or turn the hardest questions into a mock interview. The report is free.

Will the answers sound like me?

They use only facts from your resume and are short cues, not a script. You say them in your own words.

What happens to my resume?

It's read to prepare answers and kept in memory on our server while you use PrapAI. It isn't saved to our database. So you don't have to upload it again after a refresh or for your next interview, the resume text, company and job description are also kept in your own browser on this device. Signing out clears them.

How do I buy more minutes?

Sign in, then tap "Buy" on a plan. A ready email to us opens in Gmail (or your mail app) with the plan and your account email filled in: just press Send. We reply with UPI payment details and add the minutes to your account once the payment arrives. Minutes are only used while the copilot is listening, and they don't expire.

Is using it allowed in my interview?

That depends on the company. Some don't allow outside help during interviews, so check the rules for yours. PrapAI also works well for practice and mock interviews.
