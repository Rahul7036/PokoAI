I'd use two pointers moving in from both ends.

def is_palindrome(s: str) -> bool:
    chars = [c.lower() for c in s if c.isalnum()]
    left, right = 0, len(chars) - 1
    while left < right:
        if chars[left] != chars[right]:
            return False
        left, right = left + 1, right - 1
    return True  # "A man, a plan..." -> True

FAQ

Questions people ask first.

Does it work with Zoom, Google Meet and Teams?

Yes. Put your phone near the laptop speakers, or open PrapAI on the laptop and share the meeting tab with audio. PrapAI doesn't join or change your call.

What if I'm wearing headphones?

Then your phone can't hear the interviewer. Open PrapAI on the laptop, choose "Mic + meeting audio" and share the tab that has your call.

How fast are the answers?

The first words usually appear about a second after the interviewer stops talking. The rest fills in while you say the opening line.

Will the answers sound like me?

They use only facts from your resume and are short cues, not a script. You say them in your own words.

What happens to my resume?

It's read to prepare answers and kept in memory while you use PrapAI. It isn't saved to our database, and it's cleared when you upload a new one.

How do I buy more minutes?

Sign in, then tap "Buy" on a plan. A ready email to us opens in Gmail (or your mail app) with the plan and your account email filled in: just press Send. We reply with UPI payment details and add the minutes to your account once the payment arrives. Minutes are only used while the copilot is listening, and they don't expire.

Is using it allowed in my interview?

That depends on the company. Some don't allow outside help during interviews, so check the rules for yours. PrapAI also works well for practice and mock interviews.
