Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

PrapAI ("we", "us") provides an AI interview assistant at prapai.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address, full name and profession. If you sign up with email, we store your password only in hashed (encrypted, non-reversible) form.
  • Google Sign-In: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google account ID. We do not access your Gmail, contacts, Drive or any other Google data.
  • Usage information: how many seconds of the Live Copilot you have used, so we can apply your plan's time limit.
  • Verification codes: a one-time email code, stored in hashed form and deleted once your account is verified.
  • Content you provide: your resume (PDF), job descriptions and company name, which you upload to use the Copilot, Prep-Deck and Resume Matcher features.
  • Audio: when you start the Live Copilot, audio from your microphone is streamed to our server so it can be transcribed.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and secure your account and to let you sign in.
  • To transcribe interview audio and generate suggested answers, prep cards and resume feedback.
  • To track usage against your time limit.
  • To send you account emails, such as verification codes.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How your content is processed and stored

  • Your account information and usage time are stored in our database.
  • Resumes, job descriptions, audio and transcripts are not saved to our database. They are held temporarily in server memory to provide the feature and are cleared when they are replaced by a new upload or when the server restarts.
  • Server logs may briefly contain technical details, such as your email address or short snippets of transcribed text, for troubleshooting.

4. Service providers we share data with

We share data only with providers that help us run PrapAI:

  • Google Cloud (Vertex AI / Gemini and Speech-to-Text) processes your resume, job description, audio and transcripts to produce transcriptions and AI responses.
  • Google Sign-In authenticates you if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers our verification emails.
  • Our database and hosting providers store account data and run the application.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

5. Google user data

PrapAI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your Google name and email only to create and sign in to your PrapAI account.

6. Cookies and local storage

We store a sign-in token in your browser's local storage to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your account information while your account is active. To delete your account and associated data, email us at pokoai0206@gmail.com from your account's email address, and we will delete it within 30 days.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords and access-controlled systems to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for making sure your use of PrapAI, including any audio you capture, complies with the laws and policies that apply to you and to the people you speak with.

10. Children

PrapAI is not intended for anyone under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will change the "Last updated" date above when we do.

12. Contact
